## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & CORE OBJECTIVE
Build a web-based interactive geometric drawing and pedagogical authoring studio tailored for Portuguese 10th-grade Descriptive Geometry (Geometria Descritiva A).
The application focuses exclusively on **Points (Pontos), Lines (Retas), Planes (Planos), Traces (Traços), and Intersections (Interseções)** using the **Dihedral Method (Método Diédrico / Épura)** synchronized with an interactive **3D Spatial View (Diedros)**. *(Note: Do NOT implement 3D solids or 11th-grade axonometric modes).*

Key pillars of the application:
1. **Flexible Multi-View Workspace with Full-Screen Toggles:**
   - **Split View:** Simultaneous 2D Dihedral Canvas (Épura) + 3D Spatial View (showing Frontal Plane $\varphi_0$, Horizontal Plane $\nu_0$, and dihedral quadrants I, II, III, IV) + Right Sidebar.
   - **Single-View Full Screen Mode:** Dedicated one-click toolbar buttons to maximize ONLY the **2D Dihedral View (Preferred Insertion Mode)** or ONLY the **3D Spatial View** to full screen, plus an independent toggle to collapse/expand the Right Sidebar.
2. **Dual Authoring Workflow (Visual Canvas + "Form Builder" Mode):**
   - **Visual Dihedral Insertion:** Click directly on the 2D Dihedral sheet (with $0.5\text{ cm}$ grid snapping) to place $A_1$ (abcissa $x$, afastamento $y$) and $A_2$ (cota $z$) along the same vertical reference line (linha de chamada).
   - **Structured Form Builder (Modo Construtor por Blocos):** A dynamic form interface where the teacher adds modular blocks via pull-down menus (`Ponto`, `Reta`, `Plano`, `Interseção`), defines exact decimal coordinates or relational dependencies (e.g., `Reta a = União(Ponto A, Ponto B)`), and watches both the drawing and the formal text statement update in real time.
3. **Bidirectional Highlighting & Inspector:** Clicking any projection ($A_1, A_2, r_1, r_2, f_\alpha, h_\alpha$) on the canvas highlights the entire geometric entity and its card in the Right Sidebar, and vice-versa.
4. **Pedagogical Statement Generator (`Dado` vs. `Objetivo` Checkboxes):** Teachers use checkboxes on each entity/coordinate to mark what is **Given in the Statement (Dado)** versus what is the **Target Goal/Unknown (Objetivo a Determinar)**. The app automatically writes the formal Portuguese exam-style question.
5. **Simultaneous 1:1 Real-Scale SVG Export (Student + Teacher Key):** Workspace spans from $-15\text{ cm}$ to $+15\text{ cm}$ ($30\text{ cm} \times 30\text{ cm}$ total). Exports print-ready SVGs with physical `mm` dimensions (`300mm x 300mm` or cropped A4) for 1:1 scale printing on A3/A4, generating both the **Student Worksheet SVG** and the **Teacher Solution SVG** simultaneously.

## 2. MATHEMATICAL ENGINE & PORTUGUESE GDA CONVENTIONS (CRITICAL)
### 2.1 Coordinate Notation
Every Point $P$ in space is defined in centimeters by `P (x; y; z)` using a semicolon separator:
- **$x$ (Abcissa):** Position along the reference axis $x$ (Linha de Terra) relative to Origin $O(0; 0; 0)$.
  - **CRITICAL PORTUGUESE NORM:** Positive $+x$ goes to the **LEFT** of Origin $O$. Negative $-x$ goes to the **RIGHT** of Origin $O$.
- **$y$ (Afastamento / Depth):** Distance to the Frontal Projection Plane ($\varphi_0$).
  - Positive $+y$ = Anterior (in front of $\varphi_0$).
  - Negative $-y$ = Posterior (behind $\varphi_0$).
- **$z$ (Cota / Height):** Distance to the Horizontal Projection Plane ($\nu_0$).
  - Positive $+z$ = Superior (above $\nu_0$).
  - Negative $-z$ = Inferior (below $\nu_0$).

### 2.2 Exact 2D Dihedral Projection Mapping (Épura — Rebatimento)
When the Horizontal Plane ($\nu_0$) is rotated $90^\circ$ onto the Frontal Plane ($\varphi_0$) around the $x$ axis, the anterior horizontal semi-plane rotates **DOWN** and the posterior semi-plane rotates **UP**.
Therefore, for any point $A(x; y; z)$, its two projections on the 2D SVG Canvas (where `(originX, originY)` is the center of the sheet `[0;0;0]`, SVG `+X` points right, and SVG `+Y` points down) MUST be computed as:
- **Horizontal Reference Line (Eixo $x$):** Horizontal line passing through `originY`, labeled with `x` on the left end and a small tick mark at `originX` labeled `O`.
- **Line of Recall (Linha de Chamada):** Vertical line at `svgX = originX - (x * scalePxPerCm)`.
- **Horizontal Projection $A_1(x; y)$:**
  - `svgX_A1 = originX - (x * scalePxPerCm)`
  - `svgY_A1 = originY + (y * scalePxPerCm)` *(Positive $+y$ goes DOWN; Negative $-y$ goes UP)*
- **Frontal Projection $A_2(x; z)$:**
  - `svgX_A2 = originX - (x * scalePxPerCm)`
  - `svgY_A2 = originY - (z * scalePxPerCm)` *(Positive $+z$ goes UP; Negative $-z$ goes DOWN)*

### 2.3 3D Spatial View Mapping (Vista Espacial nos Diedros)
- Render using Three.js or an interactive 3D SVG/Canvas projection with orbit controls:
  - Plane $\varphi_0$ (Frontal, vertical) and Plane $\nu_0$ (Horizontal) intersecting along Axis $X$ (pointing left for $+x$).
  - Optional toggle for Bisector Planes $\beta_{13}$ and $\beta_{24}$.
  - Display the spatial point $A(x, y, z)$, its orthogonal projectors to $A_1(x, y, 0)$ and $A_2(x, 0, z)$, and $A_0(x, 0, 0)$ on the $x$ axis.
  - **Pedagogical "Rebatimento" Slider ($0^\circ \to 90^\circ$):** Allows animating the rotation of $\nu_0$ (carrying $A_1$ and $r_1$) onto $\varphi_0$ so students understand how the 3D space becomes the 2D Épura.

## 3. GEOMETRIC ENTITIES & OPERATIONS (10TH GRADE SCOPE)
*(Do NOT implement 3D solids. Focus deeply on Points, Lines, Planes, Traces, and Intersections).*

1. **Points (Pontos):**
   - Represented by projections $A_1$ and $A_2$ connected by a thin vertical construction line (linha de chamada).
   - Automatic classification in the inspector (e.g., *"1.º Diedro"*, *"2.º Diedro"*, *"Pertence ao $\beta_{13}$ ($y = z$)"*, *"Pertence ao $\beta_{24}$ ($y = -z$)"*, *"No Plano Frontal $\varphi_0$ ($y=0$)"*).
2. **Lines (Retas):**
   - Defined by:
     - **2 Points:** e.g., $r = [A, B]$ (moving $A$ or $B$ dynamically updates line $r$, its projections $r_1, r_2$, and its traces).
     - **1 Point + Direction Angles:** Point $A$ + angle with $\nu_0$ / $\varphi_0$ or specific line type (Horizontal/Nível, Frontal, Fronto-horizontal, Topo, Vertical, Perfil, Passante, Oblíqua).
   - **Automatic Line Traces & Piercing Points (Traços da Reta):**
     - **Frontal Trace $F$ ($F_1, F_2$):** Intersection with $\varphi_0$ where $y = 0$. ($F_1$ lies on the $x$ axis; $F_2$ has the frontal coordinate).
     - **Horizontal Trace $H$ ($H_1, H_2$):** Intersection with $\nu_0$ where $z = 0$. ($H_2$ lies on the $x$ axis; $H_1$ has the horizontal coordinate).
     - **Optional Bisector Intersections:** Point $Q$ (intersection with $\beta_{13}$, where $y = z$, i.e., $r_1$ and $r_2$ have symmetric distance to $x$) and Point $I_{\beta 24}$ (intersection with $\beta_{24}$, where $y = -z$, i.e., $r_1$ and $r_2$ cross on the Épura).
3. **Planes (Planos):**
   - Named with Greek letters ($\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \theta, \omega$).
   - **All 4 Definition Methods Supported:**
     1. **3 Non-Collinear Points** ($A, B, C$).
     2. **2 Intersecting or Parallel Lines** ($r, s$).
     3. **1 Line and 1 Exterior Point** ($r, A$).
     4. **Direct Plane Traces ($f_\alpha$ and $h_\alpha$):** Defined by vertex $X_0$ on the $x$ axis (abcissa where traces meet) and angles with the $x$ axis, or points on the traces (supports Oblique, Projecting/Topo, Vertical, Horizontal/Nível, Frontal, Ramp/Passante, and Profile planes).
   - Automatically calculates and draws the **Frontal Trace ($f_\alpha$)** and **Horizontal Trace ($h_\alpha$)** on the Dihedral canvas.
4. **Geometric Intersections (Interseções):**
   - **Line $\cap$ Line (Interseção de duas retas concorrentes):** Computes and plots intersection point $I(I_1, I_2)$.
   - **Line $\cap$ Plane (Interseção de reta com plano):** Computes piercing point $I(I_1, I_2)$ (and optionally displays the auxiliary projecting plane construction lines used to find $I$).
   - **Plane $\cap$ Plane (Interseção de dois planos):** Computes the intersection line $i(i_1, i_2)$ via the intersection of their frontal traces ($F_i = f_\alpha \cap f_\beta$) and horizontal traces ($H_i = h_\alpha \cap h_\beta$).

## 4. WORKSPACE LAYOUT & INTERACTION MODES
### 4.1 Viewport & Layout Controls (Top Header Bar)
- **View Mode Switcher (Prominent Segmented Control):**
  - `[ ⊞ Vista Dividida (Épura + 3D) ]` — Default split view.
  - `[ ⛶ Épura Diédrica (Full Screen) ]` — Maximizes the 2D Dihedral canvas for precision mouse insertion and editing.
  - `[ ⛶ Espaço 3D (Full Screen) ]` — Maximizes the 3D Dihedral view.
  - `[ ⇥ Mostrar/Colapsar Painel Lateral ]` — Collapses or expands the Right Sidebar so any canvas can occupy 100% of the screen width.
- **Canvas Coordinate & Grid Specification:**
  - Workspace boundaries: **$X \in [-15, +15]\text{ cm}$** and **$Y/Z \in [-15, +15]\text{ cm}$** (Total area: $30\text{ cm} \times 30\text{ cm}$).
  - Visual mouse snapping: **$0.5\text{ cm}$ increments** (`0.5 cm` snap toggleable on/off).
  - Sidebar numeric inputs: Accept **exact decimals** (step `0.01` or `0.1`, e.g., `3.25`, `-1.5`).

### 4.2 Right Sidebar with 3 Synchronized Modes/Tabs
#### Tab 1: "Construtor por Blocos" (Structured Form Builder for Patrícia)
A form-based builder where the teacher constructs the problem formally step by step:
- Button **`+ Adicionar Elemento`** appends a new interactive block card with a **Pull-Down Selector**:
  - **Option A — `Ponto`:** Input label (`A`), exact numeric fields `x`, `y`, `z`.
  - **Option B — `Reta`:** Input label (`a` or `r`), sub-dropdown for definition mode:
    - *Por 2 Pontos:* Select two existing points from pull-downs (e.g., `Ponto A` and `Ponto B`) or create inline points.
    - *Por 1 Ponto e Direção/Tipo:* Select `Ponto A` + Line Type + angles.
  - **Option C — `Plano`:** Input Greek letter ($\alpha$), sub-dropdown for definition mode (*3 Pontos*, *2 Retas*, *Reta + Ponto*, or *Traços $f_\alpha / h_\alpha$*).
  - **Option D — `Operação / Interseção`:** Select operation (*Traços da Reta $r$*, *Interseção Reta $r \cap$ Reta $s$*, *Interseção Reta $r \cap$ Plano $\alpha$*, *Interseção Plano $\alpha \cap$ Plano $\beta$*).
- Every change in the Form Builder immediately renders on the 2D/3D canvases and updates the Statement Generator.

#### Tab 2: "Inspetor & Coordenadas" (Interactive Entity List)
- Displays all Points, Lines, Planes, and Intersections.
- Clicking any entity in the 2D Dihedral Canvas or 3D View highlights its card here; clicking the card highlights its projections ($A_1, A_2$, lines, traces) on the canvas.
- Dragging $A_1$ or $A_2$ on the 2D canvas updates the numeric coordinates in real time (keeping $A_1$ and $A_2$ locked to the same $x$ abcissa when dragged horizontally).

#### Tab 3: "Gerador de Enunciado" (Pedagogical Statement & Omission Engine)
- **Per-Element & Per-Coordinate Role Checkboxes (Available on every block/card):**
  1. `[x] Dado no Enunciado` (Include in Given Data): Controls whether the element's coordinates are explicitly stated to the student and drawn in the Student Worksheet SVG. Individual coordinate checkboxes (`[x] x`, `[x] y`, `[x] z`) allow omitting a single coordinate (e.g., giving $A(3; ?; 4)$ belonging to a plane).
  2. `[🎯] Objetivo a Determinar (Pedir no Enunciado)`: Marks this element or property (e.g., Line $a$, Frontal Trace $F_a$, Plane $\alpha$'s traces, or Intersection $I$) as the **problem goal** that the student must construct.
     - When marked as `[🎯 Objetivo]`, the element is **hidden from the Student SVG** (kept only in the Teacher Solution SVG) and the text generator automatically formulates the question.
- **Auto-Generated Portuguese Statement Example:**
  - If Point $A(3; 5; 4)$ and Point $B(-1; 2; -5)$ are marked as `[x] Dado`, and Line $a = [A, B]$ + its Frontal Trace $F$ are marked as `[🎯 Objetivo]`, the generator outputs:
    > *"Determine as projeções e o traço frontal $F$ da reta $a$, definida pelos pontos $A(3; 5; 4)$ e $B(-1; 2; -5)$."*
- Includes an editable preview box, a `Copiar Enunciado` button, and a `Descarregar Pack (Aluno + Professor)` button.

## 5. PRINT-READY 1:1 REAL-SCALE SVG EXPORTER
### 5.1 Physical Dimensions & Scale
- The exported `.svg` files must use physical millimeter dimensions so they print at an exact **1:1 real scale** ($1\text{ unit} = 1\text{ cm} = 10\text{ mm}$):
  - Default sheet: `width="300mm" height="300mm" viewBox="-150 -150 300 300"` (spanning $-15\text{ cm}$ to $+15\text{ cm}$ in both axes, with `(0,0)` at Origin $O$), plus an optional **A4 Portrait/Landscape crop preset (`210mm x 297mm`)**.

### 5.2 Line Style & Weight Hierarchy (Strictly Continuous vs. Dashed)
All lines in the SVG and viewport must strictly use either **Continuous (`traço contínuo`)** or **Dashed/Interrupted (`traço interrompido`)** styles, organized into clean `<g>` layers:
1. **Eixo $x$ e Origem $O$ (`<g id="axis-x">`):** Continuous medium line (`0.35mm`, `#000000`).
2. **Linhas de Chamada e Construções Auxiliares (`<g id="construction">`):** Continuous thin (`0.15mm`) or dashed thin (`0.15mm`, `stroke-dasharray="1.5,1.5"`).
3. **Projeções de Dados e Elementos Secundários (`<g id="projections">`):** Continuous (`0.30mm`) or dashed (`0.30mm`, `stroke-dasharray="3,2"`) when representing invisible/auxiliary segments.
4. **Solução / Objeto Final (`<g id="solution">`):** Continuous thick (`0.60mm`) or dashed medium-thick (`0.45mm`, `stroke-dasharray="4,2"`).
5. **Nomenclatura / Labels (`<g id="labels">`):** Clean geometric sans-serif typography (`font-family: Inter, Arial, sans-serif`) with proper subscripts ($A_1, A_2, F_1, F_2, H_1, H_2, r_1, r_2, f_\alpha, h_\alpha$).

### 5.3 Simultaneous Dual Export ("Exportar Aluno + Professor")
Clicking **`Exportar Pack (Aluno + Professor)`** generates and downloads simultaneously:
1. `exercicio_aluno.svg`: Contains the $x$ axis, origin $O$, optional grid ticks, and ONLY the elements marked as *Dados Iniciais* (if the teacher chooses to pre-print given points; otherwise just the $x$ axis and origin $O$).
2. `exercicio_professor_resolucao.svg`: Contains the complete construction, projections, traces, intersections, and labels at 1:1 scale.
3. `enunciado_gda10.txt` (or `.md`): Contains both the Student Statement (with unknowns) and the Teacher Answer Key (with exact calculated coordinates of traces/intersections).
